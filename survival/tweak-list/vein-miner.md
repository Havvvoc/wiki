# Vein miner

When mining ore veins, crouch to activate vein miner. Durability loss for each ore broken will apply to your item (unbreaking and looting is taken into account). Vein miner will not activate if you are not crouching, if your pickaxe does not have enough durability, or if you are too hungry.
